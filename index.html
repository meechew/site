What we make

Fireside Terminal

A crackling holiday fireplace for your TV, rendered entirely in living ASCII art — like a fireplace channel broadcast from 1986. Every flame is computed in real time by a heat-diffusion fire simulation; thirty-two public-domain Christmas carols are performed live on an emulated 8-bit sound chip. The fire never repeats, and the whole app runs completely offline.

  • Real-time ASCII fire simulation — no video loops
  • 32 carols synthesized live, chiptune style
  • Adjust the flame: oxygen and fuel controls
  • Terminal color themes and a CRT effect
  • No network, no ads, no accounts, no data collection
